About Dacian Diagnostics

Clinical excellence, built on process.

Every result that leaves our laboratory passes through tracked instrumentation and automated validation before it reaches a clinician or patient. That discipline is what precision diagnostics means to us.

Analyzer tracking

Hematology and chemistry analyzers are logged and calibrated on a routine schedule, with every run checked against controls.

Automated validation

Results are cross-checked before release, flagging values that fall outside expected ranges for a second review.

Doctor & walk-in friendly

We work directly with referring physicians while remaining open to walk-in patients without an appointment.

Home & corporate reach

Sample collection extends beyond our lab doors to homes and workplaces, for patients who can't easily come to us.
